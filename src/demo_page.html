Outside-world wallet

Plays a smartphone wallet anywhere on the internet. It pays a phone's Lightning address through the standard LNURL-pay flow.
  • Resolve Lightning address (/.well-known/lnurlp)
  • Request invoice from callback
  • Verify amount and description hash
  • Pay over Lightning
  • Phone owner gets an SMS
Cash out: the outside wallet creates an invoice and a phone user pays it with their PIN (Send → Lightning invoice).
